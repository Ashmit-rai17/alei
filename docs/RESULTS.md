11. REDACTION AUDIT — four methods, each finding what the others missed
   Finding: no single audit method is sufficient for legal text; layering four independent methods
   surfaced different classes of residual identifier, and each was measured.
   1) Automated scan (gate 2, 17 Oct): capitalised non-dictionary tokens surviving redaction fell from
      70,946 (Kaggle-baseline redaction) to 37,860 (7,412 distinct). A 100-token sample was ~13-15% person
      names, i.e. roughly 1,000 distinct name tokens remain in the frozen corpus — measured, not hidden.
   2) 25 passages read by hand: 15 random (seed 20261017) read by Ayush before anything was shared —
      zero complainant names; residual items were place names, hospital record numbers (OP No., IP)
      and one stray initial. 10 further passages (seed 20261018) read during development: 5 leaks
      found and fixed in the redactor (a surname, a quoted name, a 'father of' name, a hospital name,
      an initial). Place names are kept deliberately (they carry no identity on their own); hospital
      record numbers and the stray initial remain in the frozen corpus.
   3) Blind labeller, award by award (Prashant, phase 2, 64 awards, v1-redacted text): 22 of 64 (34%) carry at least one unredacted identifier:
      {'policy number or prefix': 17, 'hospital or clinic name': 8, 'city, town, village or locality': 4, 'person name': 1, 'other identifier (registration no., bank branch)': 2}; over-redaction flagged once.
   4) Structural scan, prompted by (3): in form-style awards the policy number sits several lines
      below the 'Policy No:' label (e.g. a Star Health number in the 2021 health book), which the
      redactor never looked at. Policy-number-shaped values under the label (dates, month-years and
      round amounts excluded): 357 awards / 662 values. Masked at display time for everything that
      leaves the project (explorer, demo package): 754 values in 417 awards. Residual after masking: 23 awards / 32 values,
      every one inspected being a rupee amount written as 'Rs.126896/-' — no residual policy numbers
      found. The frozen corpus file itself is unchanged.
